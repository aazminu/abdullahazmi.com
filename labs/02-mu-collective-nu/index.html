Midwest Uncertainty Collective

 

We are a research lab working at the intersection of visualization and data-driven inference and prediction. Our mission is to empower people to learn from data by developing visual representations, approaches to uncertainty quantification, and human-in-the-loop tools that align with downstream goals and how people think. Topics we like include sampling-oriented uncertainty visualizations, visualization literacy, trust and personalization of predictions, usable statistics, and Bayesian inference.
