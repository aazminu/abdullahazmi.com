Public Grant Abstract · 6–12 Month Roadmap

Grounded Multi-Agent Reasoning over Cross-Disciplinary Scientific Literature and Biomolecular Databases

01 · Problem & Bottleneck

Rapidly expanding preprint and biomedical repositories create a severe synthesis bottleneck: existing retrieval-augmented pipelines rely on shallow abstract embeddings that fail to trace multi-hop citation provenance, verify mathematical formulations against source code, or cross-reference textual claims against structured genomic and molecular databases.

02 · Approach & Antigravity Integration

We combine hybrid dense-sparse vector retrieval (PostgreSQL/pgvector) and Neo4j citation-concept knowledge graphs with parallel agentic workflows in Google Antigravity powered by Gemini 4 Argon's 1M-token context and DeepMind Science Skills (ArXiv, OpenAlex, PubMed, AlphaGenome, and UniProt) to autonomously audit empirical claims and execute reproducible verification pipelines.

03 · Expected 6–12 Month Deliverables

Over the next 6–12 months, we will release an open-source multi-agent scientific synthesis framework (`PaperPulse v2`), a curated benchmark dataset of cross-paper claim verification graphs, custom Google Antigravity Science skills, and a peer-reviewed manuscript on grounded agentic literature discovery.
