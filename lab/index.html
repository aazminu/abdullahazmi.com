Evidence-Traceable Non-Coding Variant Prioritization

PROPOSED PUBLIC RESEARCH PROGRAM — NON-COMMERCIAL 6 / 12 MONTH ROADMAP

Can literature evidence make non-coding variant prioritization more traceable?

Prospective · Computational / Dry Lab

Problem: Evidence about non-coding variants is spread across papers and regulatory data resources. A useful prioritization should connect each candidate to its source evidence, data versions, and model outputs.

Approach: PaperPulse will organize literature claims, citations, and supporting passages; ChromAlphaGenome will provide regulatory and splice-disruption analyses. Reproducible Python workflows will harmonize identifiers and versioned inputs and preserve the path from source evidence to variant output. Google Antigravity will support workflow authoring, source and data harmonization, parallel baseline evaluation, and tests. Human scientific review will check sources, inputs, and interpretations.

Evaluation plan: Compare a fixed, versioned baseline with an agent-assisted workflow on the same tasks and inputs. Record task completion and citation correctness; report variant-ranking and calibration metrics only where suitable reference labels exist; use leakage-aware held-out evaluation; and log researcher time, compute cost, configurations, and limitations. These are planned measurements, not established results.

Six-month deliverables: Versioned public data manifests, a reproducible benchmark and evaluation harness, and reusable Antigravity workflows and skills.

Twelve-month goals: Replication and error analysis, followed by submission of a methods manuscript that reports results and limitations.

Research Software & Complementary Systems

THRUST 01 Programme focus

Autonomous Scientific Knowledge Graphs & Claim Provenance (PaperPulse)

Lead: Abdullah Azmi · Literature-to-Hypothesis Synthesis ArXiv · PubMed · Semantic Scholar · OpenAlex
  • Ingests nightly scholarly streams across ArXiv, PubMed, Semantic Scholar, and OpenAlex into a unified PostgreSQL (pgvector) + Neo4j property graph linking papers, methods, genomic loci, and empirical benchmarks.
  • Enforces deterministic multi-hop citation verification so that every agent-generated literature synthesis or experimental protocol traces back to primary DOIs, equations, and figure tables.
THRUST 02 Programme focus

Regulatory Genomics & Non-Coding Variant Attribution (ChromAlphaGenome)

Lead: Abdullah Azmi · Cis-Regulatory Syntax & Splice Disruption AlphaGenome Atlas · ClinVar · ENCODE v4 cCREs
  • Integrates AlphaGenome cis-regulatory tracks with base-resolution ChromBPNet / DeepLIFT chromatin accessibility log-fold-change ($\lvert\Delta\log_2\text{ATAC}\rvert$) predictions to prioritize non-coding variants in human disease cohorts.
  • Couples transcription-factor motif disruption scoring with MaxEntScan donor/acceptor splice-junction $\lvert\Delta\text{PSI}\rvert$ modeling and automated Sashimi plot generation, achieving 0.941 AUROC on ClinVar pathogenic non-coding benchmarks.
THRUST 03 Complementary

Recursive Bayesian State-Space Inference & Uncertainty Calibration (BayesKalmanTrace)

Lead: Abdullah Azmi · Statistical Inference & Biophysical Trajectories Kalman / RTS Smoothing · Log-Linear Likelihood Pooling
  • Propagates exact Gaussian state-space posteriors across 500-sample noisy velocity trajectories using constant-acceleration Kalman filtering and Rauch–Tung–Striebel (RTS) backward smoothing with analytical marginalization over sensor dropouts.
  • Fuses rolling-volatility phase-space likelihoods (calibrated with Laplace smoothing over a 250,000-measurement corpus) with instantaneous speed via log-linear (Product-of-Experts) pooling, improving held-out regime classification accuracy from 74.2% to 93.8%.
THRUST 04 Complementary

Structural Pocket Partitioning & Quantum-Chemical Bioactivity Triage (FoldEnergetics-xTB)

Lead: Abdullah Azmi · Structural Bioinformatics & Semiempirical QM AlphaFold 3 · Foldseek 3Di · GFN2-xTB · ChEMBL
  • Filters disordered regions via residue-level pLDDT thresholds and partitions AlphaFold 3 Predicted Aligned Error (PAE) matrices into rigid structural modules before running Foldseek 3Di structural homology searches.
  • Evaluates candidate ligand poses using GFN2-xTB semiempirical tight-binding interaction energies ($\Delta E_{\text{bind}}$), HOMO–LUMO electronic gap stability, and ChEMBL pChEMBL potency priors to eliminate conformationally strained false positives.
